Mere Sabse Pyaare Babu ❤️,

Kabhi-kabhi sochti hoon... kitni ajeeb baat hai na?

Ek waqt tha jab tum sirf ek naam the... ek photo the... ek rishta jiske baare mein ghar wale baat kar rahe the. Hum dono ek doosre ke liye bilkul anjaan the. Na tum mujhe jaante the, na main tumhe.

Aur aaj...

Aaj tum wahi insaan ho jiske bina meri har khushi adhuri lagti hai. 9 December 2023 jb hum pehli baar mile, mujhe bilkul nahi pata tha ki saamne khada woh ladka ek din meri poori duniya ban jayega. Na mujhe pata tha ki tumhari muskurahat meri favourite jagah ban jayegi... aur tumhara saath meri sabse badi taqat.

Hamari kahani kisi movie ki tarah pehli nazar ke pyaar se shuru nahi hui thi. Hamari kahani trust se shuru hui thi... aur dheere-dheere har chhoti si baat, har hasi, har conversation, har care aur har mushkil ne is rishte ko itna gehra bana diya ki aaj lagta hai jaise tum hamesha se meri zindagi ka hissa the.

Tumhari sabse pyari baat ki tum hmko itna tak jante ho ki kuchh bolna nahi padta. abhi tk es safar me bahut sare utar-chadhav aaye lekin tum mera sath hmesa diye... har vo chij ke liye hme support karna mere tarf se bolna me khyal rakhna...

Mujhe tum mein sirf ek husband nahi mila... Ek best friend mila. Ek safe place mila. Ek aisa insaan mila jiske saath main bina kisi darr ke apni asli wali "main" ban sakti hoon.

Phir 20 January 2024 ko hum engaged hue... Aur 24 April 2024 ko hum officially ek ho gaye. Us din se lekar aaj tak har din tumhare saath aur bhi khoobsurat lagta hai.

Aur phir hamari zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat chapter shuru hua... 17 February 2025 ko jab hamara chhota sa prince is duniya mein aaya. Us din tumhe Papa bante dekhkar mujhe tumse aur bhi zyada pyaar ho gaya. Mujhe uss pal ehsaas hua ki maine sirf ek perfect husband hi nahi, balki apne bete ke liye duniya ke best Papa bhi paaye hain. ❤️

Happy Birthday, Mere Babu. ❤️

Thank you har muskurahat ke liye... Har hug ke liye... Har chhoti si care ke liye... Har uss pal ke liye jab tumne bina kahe mera haath thaam liya.

Aur sabse zyada... Thank you meri zindagi ka woh hissa banne ke liye jiske bina ab main apni duniya soch bhi nahi sakti.

I love you... aaj bhi, kal bhi, aur hamesha. ❤️

Always Yours,
Sandhya ❤️
